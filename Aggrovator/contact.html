Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the disease detection?

Our AI model has an accuracy rate of 85-90% for common crop diseases. The system is continuously learning and improving. However, we recommend consulting with local agricultural experts for severe cases or when in doubt.

Is the weather data reliable?

We use multiple weather data sources including IMD, AccuWeather, and local meteorological stations to provide accurate forecasts. Data is updated every hour and has a 95% accuracy rate for 24-hour forecasts.

How often are market prices updated?

Market prices are updated in real-time during market hours (9 AM - 5 PM) and reflect the latest trading information from major agricultural markets across India including APMC mandis and e-NAM platform.

Is the platform free to use?

Basic features including weather forecasts, market prices, and basic disease detection are free for all farmers. Premium features like advanced analytics, personalized recommendations, and expert consultations require a subscription starting at ₹299/month.
