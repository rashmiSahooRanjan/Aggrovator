Crop Management

Pesticides are chemical or biological substances used to control pests that damage crops. Effective and safe Crop Management helps improve yield while protecting the environment and human health.

How to Use Pesticides Safely

  • Read and follow label instructions carefully.
  • Wear protective gear like gloves, masks, and boots.
  • Do not spray during windy conditions to avoid drift.
  • Store pesticides away from food and animal feed.
  • Dispose of containers responsibly after use.

Pros

  • Increases crop yield and productivity.
  • Prevents spread of harmful pests and diseases.
  • Reduces labor cost compared to manual pest removal.

Cons

  • Overuse can cause soil and water pollution.
  • Can harm beneficial insects and animals.
  • Improper handling may cause health hazards.
